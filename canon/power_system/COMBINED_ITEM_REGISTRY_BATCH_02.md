## Essential canon safeguards

- This is approval of **item selection**, not of abilities. These 20 entries must not be used as finalized powers until separately approved.
- Each FINAL unique lineage can have only one living owner in the world at a time. One person may own multiple different lineages.
- After the holder dies, each lineage reappears randomly in someone else under the as-yet-detailed reincarnation rules.
- An upgrade stage is not an additional separately owned power if the lineage link is later approved.
- Actual mastery belongs to an individual holder and must be recorded per character/timeline. No universal numeric cooldown or use count may be invented; preserve explicitly approved exceptions for existing powers (BKB 7-second reuse and Divine Rapier daily allowances and lifespan overuse).
- Before counting final lineages, resolve upgrade links, and check against prior Base and Combined registry entries.
