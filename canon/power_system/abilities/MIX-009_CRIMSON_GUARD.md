# Crimson Guard — ปราการโลหิต

## Approval
**APPROVED**: the user approved the proposed core concept in Combined Abilities Batch 03, without amendments. This approval does NOT approve unconfirmed evolution lines or unmentioned numerical parameters.

## Manifestation type
Group Defense

## Core ability and activation
Manifest a crimson shield or barrier extending protection to nearby people against physical attacks.

## Mastery progression
Masters cover more people, position shields precisely, and sustain stronger protection.

## Limits and counterplay
Protection may be spread thinly across many recipients; it does not negate all physical harm.
