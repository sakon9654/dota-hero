# Divine Rapier — ศัสตราเทวะ

## Decision
**USER-CONFIRMED DESIGN DIRECTION; FULL ABILITY PENDING APPROVAL.**

## Core design direction from user
- Divine Rapier is an **exceptionally destructive high-tier personal power**, extremely difficult to withstand.
- **Normal use limit: one activation per day.**
- A wielder who trains exceptionally hard **may achieve up to two activations per day**, contingent on their documented mastery and progression. Do not automatically grant two uses to a new holder.
- Divine Rapier's destructive power **can be combined with / channeled through special Hero Artifacts**, allowing power-and-equipment synergy.
- Exact combined effects depend on the particular Artifact; none are created or approved in this document.
- **No negative side effect** from Divine Rapier itself when activated within the current wielder's trained daily allowance.
- **Exceeding the wielder's daily allowance is possible, but consumes the wielder's lifespan.** The exact amount of lifespan lost per extra use is not specified and must not be invented.
- The standard allowance is one use per day; exceptionally trained wielders may attain two uses per day without lifespan cost. Uses beyond the holder-specific allowance incur the lifespan cost.

## Boundaries
- The very high destructive ceiling does not itself specify invulnerability, auto-hit, unavoidable attack, or guaranteed victory.
- Activation method, manifestation form, range, specific lifespan cost for overuse, counterplay, rules for simultaneous artifact effects, and what counts as a reset of a 'day' need later design.
- Keep holder-specific mastery records and actual activations in encounter/timeline logs; the normal one-use allowance and possible trained two-use allowance, plus the lifespan cost of exceeding the allowance, are explicit rules.

## Status
REVISION_REQUIRED: lifespan-cost rule confirmed; full manifestation awaits approval. Never add fatigue or bodily backlash for ordinary permitted uses without user approval.
