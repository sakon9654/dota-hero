# Scythe of Vyse — คำสาปแปรสภาพ

## Approval
**APPROVED**: the user approved the proposed core concept in Combined Abilities Batch 03, without amendments. This approval does NOT approve unconfirmed evolution lines or unmentioned numerical parameters.

## Manifestation type
Transformation / Curse

## Core ability and activation
Summon a staff or scepter with a monstrous head, point it at a visible living target and trigger a temporary transformation into a small animal.

## Mastery progression
At low mastery the user needs a casting interval and maintained sight; advanced users transform faster and hold the curse longer.

## Limits and counterplay
The transformed target remains conscious; suitable resistance can oppose the curse.
