# Nullifier — บัญญัติลบล้าง

## Decision
**APPROVED:** Core ability to temporarily nullify or suppress qualifying effects originating from personal powers or Hero Artifacts.

## Approved core mechanics
- Nullifier can **nullify or suppress effects** arising from a person's innate power or from special Hero Artifacts/equipment.
- Example: Nullifier used against an **Aeon Disk** power holder may prevent that target from using Aeon Disk's protective effect **for a period of time**.
- Unlike simply removing an existing positive buff, this power can temporarily **suppress access to a qualifying effect**.
- This suppression is **temporary**, not destruction or permanent transfer of the victim's unique power lineage.
- Exact duration, activation, targeting, resistance/counters, and breadth of suppression are pending detailed design and holder-specific mastery; this does not invalidate approval of the core mechanic.

## Important boundary for future balance
Do not automatically infer that Nullifier removes every ability permanently, suppresses all simultaneous abilities, or destroys Hero Artifacts. Those cases require explicit later approval.

## Continuity
Track suppressed effects and the duration per encounter in the scene state; distinguish Nullifier's influence from the target's actual power ownership.

## Status
APPROVED core ability. Do not invent unapproved activation or exact times.
