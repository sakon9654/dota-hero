# Manta Style — ร่างแยกมายา

## Approval
**APPROVED**: the user approved the proposed core concept in Combined Abilities Batch 03, without amendments. This approval does NOT approve unconfirmed evolution lines or unmentioned numerical parameters.

## Manifestation type
Illusion / Physical Duplication

## Core ability and activation
Create tangible duplicates resembling the holder, capable of interacting, moving and fighting.

## Mastery progression
Masters control more duplicates and give them more differentiated orders.

## Limits and counterplay
Duplicates are less durable and do not automatically reproduce every innate power the holder possesses.

## Canon safeguards
- This power lineage can have only one living holder at a time; the same person may hold distinct lineages.
- Ability performance, number of simultaneous effects, duration, control, recovery and precision may depend on the holder's demonstrated training, innate aptitude, condition and timeline, except when a separate explicit immutable rule is approved.
- Do not invent a universal cooldown, range, duration, or power value.
- Do not treat preliminary evolution examples as approved lineage identity, unlock methods or automatic new powers.
- Record power knowledge, combat incidents and achieved techniques for each owner separately in their character timeline.
