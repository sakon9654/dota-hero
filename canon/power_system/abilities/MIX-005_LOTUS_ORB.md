# Lotus Orb — บัวสะท้อนมนตรา

## Approval
**APPROVED**: the user approved the proposed core concept in Combined Abilities Batch 03, without amendments. This approval does NOT approve unconfirmed evolution lines or unmentioned numerical parameters.

## Manifestation type
Reflection / Support

## Core ability and activation
Create an energy lotus and place it on the holder or an ally; when that person is targeted by a qualifying power, the lotus creates a reflected copy of the effect against its source.

## Mastery progression
Masters sustain more lotuses, control timing and guard several allies.

## Limits and counterplay
Reflecting does not necessarily cancel the original effect; it does not reflect every area attack.
