# Aghanim's Shard — ชื่อพลังรอกำหนด

## Decision
**REVISION_REQUIRED:** core ability not approved; user revisions below are design instructions pending review.

## Ability design
Previous self-only power enhancement mechanism is NOT approved. User correction: the ability can **substantially enhance the special equipment/artifacts** designed from Dota 2 hero abilities. **User-confirmed activation condition:** Artifact enhancement is active **only while the holder is physically holding the relevant Hero Artifact**. Upon releasing, dropping or losing hold of the equipment, the enhancement stops; it must not persist on unattended equipment. The broader enhancement mechanism remains in REVISION_REQUIRED pending review. Compatibility, exact effects, enhancement ceiling, costs, risks and balancing interactions must be designed and approved. Do not assume it enhances innate item-based powers.

## Confirmed simultaneous amplification rule (2026-11-01)
- The Shard wielder can enhance **multiple Hero Artifacts simultaneously**, provided **each affected artifact is currently held by that wielder**.
- Enhancement applies independently to every qualifying held artifact; there is no one-artifact-only restriction.
- If an artifact is dropped, released, thrown, or taken out of the wielder's hands, enhancement on **that artifact** ceases immediately; other still-held artifacts remain eligible.
- Do not impose a universal maximum count, duration, cooldown, or power multiplier without later approval. Physical ability to hold an artifact and individual mastery still apply.
- The rule does **not** grant enhancement to items merely carried in bags, worn, or held by another person. Whether unconventional holding methods count remains undecided.
- Detailed enhancements for each Hero Artifact and the broader core ability are still in design; **ability_status remains REVISION_REQUIRED** until the full concept is approved.

## Individual Mastery Rule
The core ability belongs to its power lineage. Actual output, precision, effective range, sustain, control and reuse depend on the current user's training, aptitude, physical/mental condition and documented mastery at the relevant point in the story timeline. Do **not** hardcode universal cooldowns or durations. Do not transfer predecessor mastery automatically to a new holder.

## Continuity
Writers must check the holder's mastery timeline, current power ownership, known techniques and knowledge states before writing a scene. This record does not approve undisclosed evolutions.
