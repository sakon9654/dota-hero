# Quelling Blade — คมตัดพิฆาต

## Decision
**APPROVED:** core ability concept only; individual-specific mastery governs implementation.

## Ability design
The holder channels an enhanced cutting property through a held sharp weapon and must actually execute a cutting motion. Control and skill can improve precision and ability to identify weak seams in materials. It requires an appropriate strike and access to a target; it is not remote destruction at will. Cutting magic or supernatural forces remains **unapproved** and must not be inferred.

## Individual Mastery Rule
The core ability belongs to its power lineage. Actual output, precision, effective range, sustain, control and reuse depend on the current user's training, aptitude, physical/mental condition and documented mastery at the relevant point in the story timeline. Do **not** hardcode universal cooldowns or durations. Do not transfer predecessor mastery automatically to a new holder.

## Continuity
Writers must check the holder's mastery timeline, current power ownership, known techniques and knowledge states before writing a scene. This record does not approve undisclosed evolutions.
