# Ghost Scepter — ร่างวิญญาณ

> **Approved ability concept.** Timings, reuse and mastery are individual-dependent; no universal numeric values are canon.

## Approved manifestation and core effect
- The wielder can transition their body into a translucent, ghostlike or ethereal state.
- Ordinary physical weapon attacks pass through the transformed body.
- Certain magical or supernatural effects can still interact with and hurt the wielder.
- While transformed, the wielder cannot perform ordinary physical weapon attacks in the usual manner.
- The ability is part of the person, not dependent on holding a Ghost Scepter item.
- The transition uses an inward concentration / brief breath-control gesture as the **currently approved draft activation**; exact presentation may be revised when character profiles are written.
- Prolonged or repeated usage may disturb the wielder's senses, with magnitude varying by individual.

## Explicit user adjustment
- The length of each transformation depends on the **particular person**: it may be longer or shorter.
- How soon a character can activate Ghost Scepter again also depends on the **person**, rather than a fixed cooldown.
- Remove prior universal **5-second duration** and **15-second reuse** values.

## Individual mastery profiles
Store per-character details for actual sustainable duration, recovery, control, mental/physical cost, and known counters. Do not infer identical numbers across successive owners.

## Pending design, not included in approval
- Ghost Scepter to Ethereal Blade evolution mechanics and requirements.
- Exact spell damage types that affect the ethereal body.
- Specific activation variations, exact costs and numeric character limits.

## Continuity enforcement
Each holder's profile and mastery timeline determine how long they can remain ethereal and when they can reuse this power; do not silently apply the previous holder's capacity to a new one.

## Decision
**GHOST SCEPTER ABILITY: APPROVED (core concept, with individual-dependent timing).**
