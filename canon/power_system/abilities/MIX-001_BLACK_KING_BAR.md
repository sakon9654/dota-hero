# Black King Bar — ราชันไร้มนตรา

## Decision
**APPROVED** by user. The previous proposed core ability concept is accepted with the user's explicit corrections below.

## Manifestation
- The holder must **hold a golden club** to invoke the defensive manifestation.
- Activation grants temporary resistance to supernatural interference, including eligible enchantment, control, and involuntary transformations.
- It does not automatically make the holder immune to ordinary physical impact or all possible power effects.
- Being disarmed before activation can stop them from invoking the power.

## Timing: mixed fixed and individual rules
- **Active duration is individual-dependent**: training, control, aptitude and current condition determine how long the protective state can be maintained.
- **Fixed re-use rule: 7 seconds** for this power, irrespective of individual mastery. Individual mastery must not silently override this canonical seven-second rule.
- Exact start point for measuring those seven seconds (e.g. activation vs after the preceding effect ends) is **not yet specified**; explicitly resolve it before writing a scene that depends on this difference.

## Pending
Exact immunity/resistance interaction matrix, the nature of the golden club (physical or conjured), and activation/counter details require dedicated later design.

## Continuity
Holder-specific mastery defines duration, while the 7-second rule remains immutable unless user approves an exception.
