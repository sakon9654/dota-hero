# Dagon — ลำแสงพิฆาต

## Approval
**APPROVED**: the user approved the proposed core concept in Combined Abilities Batch 03, without amendments. This approval does NOT approve unconfirmed evolution lines or unmentioned numerical parameters.

## Manifestation type
Directed Energy

## Core ability and activation
Accumulate energy in a hand or chosen body part and discharge a destructive beam toward a target.

## Mastery progression
Improve intensity, speed, accuracy and energy control. Dagon levels 1–5 are progression within the same lineage, not separate owners.

## Limits and counterplay
Aiming and control are required; attacks can be avoided or defended against. Upgrade requirements are not approved.

- Do not invent a universal cooldown, range, duration, or power value.
