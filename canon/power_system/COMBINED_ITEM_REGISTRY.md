## Lineage and duplication checks

1. **Battle Fury / Quelling Blade:** Battle Fury builds from Quelling Blade in Dota 2. Quelling Blade (BASE-004) already has an approved core concept. Flag MIX-020 as a **candidate evolution**, not an automatic new unique power. Final lineage classification requires user approval.
2. **Shiva's Guard / Orb of Frost:** Both have a cold/frost theme, but thematic resemblance alone does not make the same lineage. Keep Shiva's Guard independent *provisionally* and review for ability overlap when designed. Eye of Skadi is already proposed as an Orb of Frost evolution.
3. **Silver Edge / Shadow Blade:** Silver Edge is an upgrade of Shadow Blade, which has not been approved as a base power. Keep Silver Edge as an approved *design candidate*, but defer choosing whether to introduce Shadow Blade as its base stage or design another structure.
4. **Gleipnir / Rod of Atos:** Gleipnir upgrades from Rod of Atos, not yet approved as a power; defer that lineage decision.
5. **Dagon:** Multiple shop Dagon levels form one Dagon candidate lineage, not separate new powers.
6. **Remaining candidates:** No direct overlaps identified with the 8 base-item registry entries at this stage. This is an initial overlap screen, **not** a full completed shop recipe audit.

## Governing rules

- Every *final unique lineage* has at most one living holder at a time; a person may possess multiple distinct lineages.
- Upon a holder's death, each lineage randomly reappears in another person, subject to separately approved reincarnation rules.
- An upgrade within the same lineage is **not** an additional independently held unique power.
- Exact ability performance, quantity, endurance, re-use and technique depend on an individual holder's mastery and timeline. No universal arbitrary fixed cooldowns.
- No ability design, manifestation mechanics, counters, evolution requirements, or artifact specifications may be promoted to canon merely because an item appears here.
