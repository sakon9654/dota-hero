## Scope and rules

- No ability effects, activation conditions, limitations, or balance values are approved by this list.
- Shop-combined items are the primary source of power lineages; only distinctive basic items receive explicit exceptions.
- Items that merely provide stats, common consumable healing/mana, and generic recipes are excluded.
- Each power lineage can have only one living holder worldwide at a time, although a person may hold multiple different lineages.
- After a holder dies, each power lineage randomly reappears in another person; reincarnation details remain to be designed.
- Upgrades such as Swift Blink are evolutions of Blink Dagger lineage, not separate simultaneously held powers.
- Hero abilities are inspirations for separately designed special equipment/artifacts, not innate powers.
